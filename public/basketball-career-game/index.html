Questions

Is the career mode free?

Yes. Hardwood is free in your browser, with no download, no account and no in-app purchases.

Can I create my own player?

Yes. You build your player's attributes, position and background, then get drafted by a real team in the season you choose.

How hard is it?

Pick Easy, Normal or Hard. On Hard your shots fall less often and opponents play tougher, so becoming a star takes real work.

Which seasons can I start in?

Any of the 81 seasons from 1946–47 to 2026–27, with that year's real players as your teammates and rivals.

Does it save automatically?

Yes, automatically after every game, on your device — no account needed.
