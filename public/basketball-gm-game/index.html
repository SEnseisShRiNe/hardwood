What you control as GM

The draft

Scout the real draft class all season, watch the lottery, then make your picks — or trade up for the player you want. Undrafted players become free agents.

Trades

Two- and three-team deals with players and draft picks. CPU teams value talent, age, contracts and picks, so lopsided offers get turned down.

Salary cap

Cap space, the mid-level exception, minimum deals and the luxury tax, following the rules of your era.

Free agency

Every player has priorities — winning, money, role. Some sign on the spot, some counter, some walk away.

Owner and finances

Hit your owner's mandate, keep the books in the black and protect your reputation, or you might be looking for a new job.

Seasons and playoffs

Sim game by game or skip ahead. Standings, awards races, a full playoff bracket and a champion at the end.

How a GM run goes

  • Pick a season and a team. Contender, rebuild or somewhere in between.
  • Shape the roster. Trade, sign free agents and set your rotation before opening night.
  • Play the season. Sim ahead, check the standings and make moves before the deadline.
  • Draft and re-sign. The offseason is where dynasties are built: lottery, draft night, extensions and free agency.
  • Keep going. Players age and develop, rookies arrive and teams rise and fall for as many seasons as you like.

Questions

Is Hardwood's GM mode free?

Yes. It's free to play in any browser, with no download, no account and no in-app purchases.

Are the players and rosters real?

Yes. Each of the 81 seasons from 1946–47 to 2026–27 starts with that year's real players, rosters and contracts. Team nicknames are Hardwood's own, and there is also a random-league mode with brand-new players.

Can I trade draft picks?

Yes. Picks are part of trades, including the real traded picks in recent seasons, and you can build two- and three-team deals.

How does the salary cap work?

It follows real rules for the era: cap space, a mid-level exception once you're over the cap, minimum contracts and the luxury tax. Free agents negotiate — some accept right away, some hold firm and some walk.

Does it save my progress?

Yes, automatically after every game, on your device. In a browser, clearing the site's data erases your saves, so leave it alone if you want to keep your league.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Hardwood works in any mobile browser, and you can add it to your home screen to play it like an app.
